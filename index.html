EDDIE vs RICHIE

A scrap in the flat. Hit each other with whatever you can grab.

How to play

Eddie is slower and tougher. He swings a cricket bat, and can grab and throw things too (but not as hard).

Richie is quicker but can’t take as many hits. He picks things up and throws them.

Eddie (P1)

Move: W A S D
Swing bat: F
Grab / throw: G

Richie (P2)

Move: arrow keys
Throw: K
Pick up / drop: L

On a phone, use the pad and buttons (1 player). P or Esc pauses. M turns the beeps off. Space starts a 1-player game as Eddie.

PAUSED

P or Esc to carry on.

SOMEONE WINS

Or press Space to play again.
