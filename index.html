Round one · The Flat

EDDIE VS RICHIE

A scrap in the flat. Hit each other with whatever you can grab.

How to play

First to win 2 rounds takes the match.

Eddie is slower and tougher. He swings a cricket bat, and can grab and throw things too (but not as hard).

Richie is quicker but can’t take as many hits. He picks things up and throws them.

Jump over low throws, or hop over the chair.

Eddie (P1)

Move: W A S D
Jump: Space
Swing bat: F
Grab / throw: G

Richie (P2)

Move: arrow keys
Jump: Enter
Throw: K
Pick up / drop: L

In 1 player you can use either set of keys. On a phone, use the pad and buttons. P or Esc pauses. M turns all sound off, N just the music. Space starts a 1-player game as Eddie.
